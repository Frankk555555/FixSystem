# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Primary users are university students, professors, and staff who need to report broken facilities or equipment on campus. 
Secondary users are Technicians (across Electrical, Plumbing, and General Construction departments) who receive, schedule, and complete the repair jobs, as well as Administrators who oversee the system, manage roles, and review reports.

## Product Purpose
A unified online repair request system for the university. It exists to streamline the reporting, tracking, scheduling, and resolution of campus maintenance issues. Success means a seamless flow of communication and action between those reporting issues and the technicians resolving them, reducing downtime for campus facilities.

## Positioning
A centralized, role-based platform tailored for a university environment that uniquely integrates direct chat, calendar scheduling, and automated ticket routing by department into a single cohesive workflow.

## Operating Context
Users interact with the system when they encounter a physical facility issue on campus, often requiring them to take and upload photos or videos of the problem. Technicians interact with the system to plan their workdays, communicate directly with the issue reporters, and update statuses while navigating the campus.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Ticket lifecycle must follow: Pending -> Assigned -> Scheduled -> In Progress -> Completed.
- Role-based access control for User, Technician, and Admin.
- Automated routing to 3 Technician departments: Electrical, Plumbing, Construction.
- Core features include real-time chat between reporter and technician, calendar-based scheduling, and media uploads for issue evidence.
- Stack: Built with Next.js and managed through Lovable.

## Brand Commitments
Project name: "Campus Fix Hub" (uni-repair-hub). 

## Evidence on Hand
Features and roles are thoroughly defined in `Project_Scope.md` and `README.md`. No pre-existing strict visual brand commitments have been explicitly bounded yet.

## Product Principles
1. **Clarity in reporting:** The issue reporting flow must be simple and intuitive enough for any university member to use quickly from their mobile device.
2. **Role-specific efficiency:** Technicians and admins should only see the information and actions immediately relevant to their responsibilities.
3. **Transparent resolution:** Integrated chat and clear status tracking are essential to prevent confusion and duplicate reports.
